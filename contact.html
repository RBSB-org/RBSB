QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about RBSB Events.

What is RBSB Events?

RBSB Events is a student web-development project designed to bring event discovery, venue exploration, service information and budget planning together in one place.

Can I explore different types of events?

Yes. Visit the Events page to explore categories such as weddings, birthdays, college events, corporate gatherings and other celebrations.

How can I estimate my event budget?

Open the Budget Planner page and enter estimated expenses for different event categories. Interactive calculations will be added during the JavaScript development stage.

Can I submit an event booking enquiry?

Yes. Visit the Booking page to enter your event details and preferences. In this student-project version, submitting the form does not create a real reservation.

Will the contact form send an email?

Not yet. The form is currently a front-end demonstration. Sending real messages will require a backend or a suitable form-processing service.
